# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this repository to be a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive learning
space for QA engineers, developers, students, and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but hold people accountable for harmful behavior.
- Give clear, actionable feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and learning styles.
- Focus discussions on testing, automation, AI, and project improvement.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person’s private information.
- Sharing credentials, secrets, malware, or unsafe instructions.
- Spam, self-promotion without relevance, or disruptive behavior.
- Deliberately misleading documentation or examples.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private issue or
contact the repository maintainer. Reports will be handled with care and
confidentiality where possible.
